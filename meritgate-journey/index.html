      03 / The synchronous hybrid classroom

      One international classroom. Two ways to participate.

      MeritGate combines live, synchronous hybrid teaching with Moodle-supported independent study, learning materials and assignments. The difference is where learners attend—not when they begin or which qualification they work towards.

      04 / Commitments to every learner

      Clarity. Fairness. Academic integrity.

      01

      Education comes first.

      Tuition relates to a defined educational programme. Awards follow successful assessment, not automatic enrolment.

      02

      Merit, never chance.

      Published academic criteria, assessed evidence and an appeals process. No random draw or lottery.

      03

      Clear commitments.

      Fees, scholarship inclusions, exclusions, travel conditions and academic responsibilities must be disclosed before payment.
