      03 / Commitments to every learner

      Clarity. Fairness. Academic integrity.

      01

      Education comes first.

      Tuition relates to a defined educational programme. Awards follow successful assessment, not automatic enrolment.

      02

      Merit, never chance.

      Published academic criteria, assessed evidence and an appeals process. No random draw or lottery.

      03

      Clear commitments.

      Fees, scholarship inclusions, exclusions, travel conditions and academic responsibilities must be disclosed before payment.
